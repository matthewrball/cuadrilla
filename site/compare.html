Compared with taking over the desktop

Desktop computer use moves your pointer and pauses you. Cuadrilla refuses that. It is not a second copy of Cua Driver. Cua Driver clicks and types. Cuadrilla keeps the agents from sharing one mouse.

What not to say

It does not replace the engine

Window input still goes through the Cua Driver fork. Cuadrilla does not reimplement SkyLight, accessibility, or screenshots.

Clicks do not overlap yet

cua-driver 0.44 admits one hardware action at a time. Cuadrilla overlaps scheduling. cuadrilla doctor reports parallel_hid: false until a driver build changes that.
